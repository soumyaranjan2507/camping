Koraput Hill Camping

Camp amidst the clouds at Odisha's highest regions

Camping Experience

Set up camp near Deomali Peak, Odisha's highest point at 1,672 meters. Experience cool mountain air, panoramic sunrise views, and campfires under starry skies. Perfect for trekkers and nature photographers.

Best For:

Trekking, Mountain Views, Photography, Bird Watching

Camping Highlights

  • Base camp at 1,400 meters elevation
  • Guided sunrise trek to Deomali Peak
  • Campfire with local tribal storytelling
  • Visit to nearby Duduma Waterfall
  • Organic farm-to-table meals
  • Stargazing with telescope (weather permitting)

Mahendragiri Mountain Camp

Camp at Odisha's second highest peak with mythological significance

Camping Experience

Set up camp on the slopes of Mahendragiri (1,501 meters), a peak mentioned in the Ramayana. Experience spiritual energy, ancient temples, and breathtaking valley views. The campsite offers both adventure and tranquility.

Best For:

Spiritual Retreats, Meditation, Trekking, Mythology Enthusiasts

Camping Highlights

  • Campsite with ancient temple views
  • Morning meditation sessions
  • Guided heritage trail to archaeological sites
  • Rappelling and rock climbing (optional)
  • Traditional Odia meals cooked on site
  • Campfire with devotional music

Talamali Mountain Camp

Camp at Odisha's second highest peak with mythological significance

Camping Experience

Set up camp on the tabletop of Talamali Hills, a scenic highland of southern Odisha. Experience vast green meadows, floating clouds, and panoramic valley views. The campsite offers peaceful surroundings, cool mountain air, and a perfect balance of nature and relaxation.

Spiritual Retreats, Meditation, Trekking, Mythology Enthusiasts

Your Safety is Our Priority

🛡️ Trained Guides

All our camping guides are certified in wilderness first aid, CPR, and emergency response. They have extensive knowledge of local terrain and weather patterns.

📡 Communication

Satellite phones and two-way radios available at all campsites. Regular check-ins with base camp. Emergency evacuation plans for each location.

⚕️ Medical Support

Comprehensive first aid kits at all campsites. Nearest medical facilities mapped. Guides trained in handling common wilderness emergencies.

🌦️ Weather Monitoring

Daily weather updates and contingency plans. Campsites chosen for safety from flash floods, landslides, and other natural hazards.
